Handover, duty engineer -> night desk
=====================================

The rotation note for this window is in vault.enc. It was sealed with
AES-256 in CBC mode, with a random salt, and the key was stretched from the
passphrase with two hundred and forty thousand rounds of PBKDF2 taken over
SHA-512 rather than the default digest.

The passphrase itself is in passphrase.txt. vault.sha256 is the digest of
the note as it was before sealing; check what you recover against it.

Do not re-seal the archive or edit it in place: the courier copy and this
one have to stay identical.
